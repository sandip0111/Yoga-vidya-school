Day 1 Monday, September 21 • 5:30 PM IST
Live Class
📖

History of Yoga

Prashantji Founder, Yoga Vidya School

On our first day the founder of our school will lead us from tradition to modern practice. Everyone practices yoga and follows a certain tradition but how many of us actually know where that tradition came from?

Key questions we will explore:
  • How did yoga evolve?
  • Where did the different systems of yoga come from?
  • And how did the yoga we practice today develop?

We will go beyond simply practicing yoga. We will explore the origins, history, and evolution of yoga, and understand how different traditions and practices developed over time.

Live Class

Hatha Yoga

Shivamji Traditional Hatha Teacher & AYUSH Certified Yoga Therapist

On our second day our traditional hatha teacher will lead a class for us. Shivam has studied and practiced yoga in traditional ashrams in Rishikesh, and he has also trained personally under Prashant Jakhmola Ji.

Ministry of AYUSH Certified Certified Yoga Therapist Traditional Rishikesh Lineage

He is a government-certified Yoga Therapist under the Ministry of AYUSH (Ministry of Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy), India. His approach combines traditional yogic knowledge with precise alignment and therapeutic application.

Pranayama As It Is

Archanaji Yogini from Rishikesh & Prāṇa Master

On our third day, our Yogini from Rishikesh will lead a Pranayama class for us.

Archana’s wider studies include Nāda Yoga (Sound Healing), Yin Yoga, and Pre-Natal and Post-Natal Yoga. She has also been fortunate to learn from traditional masters including Swami Taponidhi of Bihar School of Yoga and Swami Ji Kapri, who worked closely with Swami Satyananda Saraswati Ji.

Areas of Devotion & Study:
Prāṇāyāma Prāṇa Śakti Dhyāna Haṭha Yoga Mantra Kuṇḍalinī Nāda Yoga

She is particularly devoted to exploring and sharing traditional approaches to Prāṇāyāma, Prāṇa Śakti, Dhyāna, Haṭha Yoga, Mantra, and Kuṇḍalinī.
