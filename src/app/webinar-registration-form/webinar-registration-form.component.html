✨ A Free Online Webinar on the Hidden Science of Prana

Most people think they are breathing…
Yogis don’t breathe like that.

When your practice matures, it naturally becomes service. Yoga is not only about improving yourself. In the traditional path, sincere practice (Sadhana) gradually transforms into selfless sharing (Seva). This is how the wisdom of yoga has been preserved for thousands of years through practitioners who embodied the teachings before passing them on.

This webinar is for those who feel called to deepen their practice and explore the path of teaching from an authentic, traditional perspective. Whether you aspire to become a yoga teacher or simply want to understand the true purpose of yoga, this session will provide clarity and direction.

What You Will Learn

- clear Road map to become from practitioners to Teacher

  • The true meaning of Sadhana and why it is the foundation of every authentic yoga teacher.
  • Why sincere practice naturally evolves into Seva - the sharing of knowledge for the benefit of others.
  • The essential qualities of a genuine yoga teacher beyond poses and certifications.
  • How to cultivate a strong and consistent personal practice.
  • Why teaching yoga begins with inner transformation, not external techniques.
  • The difference between traditional yoga and much of what is presented as modern yoga today.
  • Why many practitioners experience techniques but not real transformation.
  • How an authentic lineage preserves the power of yoga through direct experience.
  • How sharing yoga strengthens your own practice and deepens your understanding.
  • Whether the path of teaching is the next step in your own spiritual journey.
Who Should Attend?
  • Dedicated yoga practitioners seeking a deeper understanding of yoga.
  • Students considering a Yoga Teacher Training.
  • Yoga teachers who wish to reconnect with the traditional roots of yoga.
  • Anyone who feels called to transform personal practice into meaningful service.
Why Join This Webinar?

It is an invitation to understand the traditional vision of yoga, where practice is not limited to the mat but becomes a way of living, serving, and inspiring others.

If you have ever felt that there is something deeper beyond modern yoga classes, this webinar will help you discover that path.

About the Teacher

Prashant Jakhmola is the founder of Yoga Vidya School and has dedicated his life to the study and practice of traditional yoga. His teachings are rooted in authentic yogic lineages and emphasize direct experience, disciplined practice, and the inner transformation described in the classical texts.
