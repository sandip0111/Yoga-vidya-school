Step Into the Heart of Yoga - Mysore Awaits You

Embark on a meaningful journey in Mysore, India - a city deeply connected with the history and evolution of modern yoga. Known for its rich yoga heritage and its association with legendary teachers such as Sri T. Krishnamacharya and Sri K. Pattabhi Jois, Mysore offers a unique environment to experience yoga beyond the physical practice.

Our 200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training at Yoga Vidya School is more than a certification course it is an opportunity to deepen your practice, explore traditional yogic wisdom, and develop a stronger connection with yourself.

Yoga Alliance Registered, our immersive training combines traditional knowledge with a systematic and comprehensive approach to yoga education. Guided by experienced teachers, you will explore Hatha Yoga, Ashtanga Vinyasa, Pranayama, Yoga Philosophy, Meditation, Anatomy, and the deeper principles of yogic living.

Through hands-on learning, personalised guidance, and dedicated daily practice, you will experience yoga as a complete discipline rooted in tradition yet relevant to modern life.

As a student, you will develop a deeper understanding of āsana, breath, alignment, and the philosophy behind each practice.

And as a future yoga teacher, you will gain the knowledge, confidence, and teaching skills to share yoga with clarity, authenticity, and purpose.

Come to Mysore not just to become a certified yoga teacher, but to experience the depth of yoga as a way of life.
