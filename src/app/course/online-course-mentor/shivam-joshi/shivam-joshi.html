Hatha Yoga with Props - Online Classes with Shivam Joshi

About the Classes

Deepen your Hatha Yoga practice through traditional teachings, precise alignment, and the intelligent use of props.

Join Shivam Joshi for live online Hatha Yoga classes from Monday to Thursday. Explore traditional Hatha Yoga with an emphasis on alignment, mindful practice, and the use of props to support and refine your experience.

Hatha Yoga is more than the physical execution of postures. It is a practice that invites us to develop awareness, stability, and a deeper understanding of the body and breath.

In these classes, Shivam combines the foundations of Traditional Hatha Yoga with precise alignment and the intelligent use of props. Props can help practitioners explore postures with greater awareness, understand alignment, and adapt their practice to their individual needs.

The classes offer an opportunity to establish a consistent practice, refine your understanding of asana, and learn through direct experience.

Whether you are looking to deepen your existing practice or develop a more attentive approach to Hatha Yoga, these sessions invite you to explore the practice with guidance and care.

What to Expect

  • Traditional Hatha Yoga: Explore postures through the principles of traditional yogic practice.
  • Alignment and Awareness: Develop a clearer understanding of posture, stability, and mindful movement.
  • The Use of Props: Learn how props can support your practice and help you explore postures more consciously.
  • Regular Practice: Build consistency through four live sessions each week.
  • Flexible Participation: Attend live or use the available recordings when you cannot join a session.

Meet Your Teacher: Shivam Joshi

Shivam Joshi is a Traditional Hatha Yoga Teacher with a Bachelor’s and Master’s degree in Yoga Science. He has been practicing Yoga for seven years and has studied and practiced in traditional ashrams in Rishikesh, India.

He has also had the opportunity to train personally under Prashant Jakhmola Ji, deepening his understanding of traditional yogic teachings.

Shivam is a government-certified Yoga Therapist through India’s Ministry of AYUSH (Ministry of Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy).

His specializations include Iyengar Yoga Therapy and Traditional Hatha Yoga. His teaching combines traditional yogic knowledge with precise alignment and the therapeutic application of props.
