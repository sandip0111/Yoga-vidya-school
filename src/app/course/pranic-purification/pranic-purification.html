At Yoga Vidya School, we have designed a progressive learning journey that we call the Pranic Path. It is a structured progression that takes students from the foundations of breath awareness to our most advanced Pranayama Certification: Pranic Bodh.

Why is this progression important?

Each stage builds upon the previous one. As the practices become more subtle and powerful, they require a strong foundation in both knowledge and personal experience.
